About Yunan

Coming from a small old town in eastern China, I’ve always appreciated how art and design have carried me forward. My journey began in 2012, when I left home for Shanghai to study Industrial Design at Tongji University. That first step opened my door to the globe: from exploring art at Folkwang University in Germany, to completing my MFA in Child Culture Design at Gothenburg University here in Sweden. Since 2017, I’ve grown from a student into a professional, working with brands like Opera Software, Freepower and  Sudio.

For me, design is more than a job, It is how I express myself— a passion I would follow regardless of the outcome or reward.

Besides creating, I love running, hiking, cycling and climbing. These activities give me sparkles of inspiration, whether for wild ideas or for the rhythm of daily work.
