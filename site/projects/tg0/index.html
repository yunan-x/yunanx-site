TG0

Rebranding, website design and Webflow development

Job Scope
‍To overhaul the digital presence of TG0, a platform for next-gen physical interfaces. The challenge was to translate tactile hardware technology into an immersive, interaction-driven web experience.
‍
Role
I managed the project from concept to webflow development, combining the roles of Project Manager, Art Director, designer and webflow Developer.

‍Outcome
‍Launched a dynamic, CMS-driven platform that effectively communicates the technology's unique capabilities. The cohesive rebrand and technical implementation successfully positioned TG0 as a leader in hardware innovation.

What I enjoyed most about this project was translating technical complexity into aesthetic value. I synthesized distinct elements—materials, sensors, and scripts—into a cohesive design language. This created a unique visual voice that not only demystifies the technology for the audience but also leaves a lasting brand impression.
